High-risk criteria — set "at_risk_flag": true when ANY of these is true:
1. Customer requests subscription cancellation, contract termination, refund, or threatens to churn / move to a competitor.
2. Customer reports duplicate billing, double charge, unauthorised payment, or any fraud / suspected breach.
3. Customer reports a current outage, service unavailability, or "cannot use the product at all".
4. Customer is locked out of authentication: SSO failure, account locked, MFA / 2FA blocking access, password reset entirely broken.
5. Customer raises a POPIA / GDPR data access, deletion, or correction request.
6. Customer expresses strong negative emotion AND mentions escalation to legal, regulator, media, or public complaint channels.
7. Customer reports an unresolved support ticket older than 7 days or repeated SLA breach.
8. Customer reports a webhook / API / data sync failure that is blocking their core business workflow.
9. Customer mentions large financial impact, lost revenue, or "this is costing us money".

Set "at_risk_flag": false when the message is a positive testimonial, a benign feature inquiry, a documentation lookup, a routine pricing question, or general feedback with no operational impact.

Sentiment guidance:
- "sentiment_polarity" must independently reflect emotional tone, regardless of risk: -1.0 = strongly negative, 0.0 = neutral, +1.0 = strongly positive.

Support.General triage (read this before using any Support.General.* label):
- Use "Support.General.PositiveFeedback" when the customer is praising the product, thanking the team, sharing a testimonial, or expressing satisfaction with no operational issue.
- Use "Support.General.Inquiry" when the customer is asking a benign question (pricing, features, plan details, support hours, documentation, policy, sandbox availability, integration availability, scheduled maintenance) with no complaint and no broken workflow.
- Use "Support.General.Unknown" ONLY when the text is gibberish, completely off-topic for a SaaS product, or genuinely impossible to map to ANY other category. Never use Unknown as a convenient fallback for messages that are clearly praise, clearly an inquiry, or clearly map to a more specific domain like Billing / Authentication / Performance / Integration / Reporting / Compliance.

Rules:
- If uncertain about the taxonomy label, still choose the closest single label from the list — never invent one.
- Apply high-risk criteria literally — do not soften the flag just because the text is polite.
- Keep reason_short concise and factual.
- Be robust to South African English, Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa, and mixed slang wording.
